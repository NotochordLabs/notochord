# Source

Ryan K, Lu Z, Meinertzhagen IA (2016). The CNS connectome of a tadpole larva of Ciona intestinalis (L.) highlights sidedness in the brain of a chordate sibling. eLife 5:e16962. https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.16962 License: CC BY 4.0.

`nodes.csv` and `edges.csv` are built from the paper's Figure 16, source data 1 matrix with `scripts/build-connectome.py` (`paper/fig16-data1.xlsx`). Muscle columns and totals are removed.

Do NOT use the `cintestinalis` copy on Netzschleuder: in the file downloaded on 2 November 2026 the row labels were shifted against the paper (192 of 205 rows carried another cell's name).

`paper/fig3-data1.xlsx`: the paper's cell table, used for `cell-map.csv`.
